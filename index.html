Start it

  • Windows

    Unzip the whole folder and run CivilianProtocol.exe. If a blue "Windows protected your PC" box appears, click More info, then Run anyway. You see it because the build isn't signed yet.

  • Mac

    Unzip it and move Civilian Protocol to Applications. The first time, right-click the app, choose Open, then Open again. If your Mac still refuses, allow it under System Settings, Privacy & Security.

  • Linux

    Unzip it, then run CivilianProtocol.x86_64.

What it is

You live on a street in the Florida Keys, two years after an eleven-day war nobody can explain. You take a boat out to islands that were left behind, find what you can, and try to get home with it. What you bring back builds your house, feeds the people in it and makes you better at the things you do.

  • Play alone, or meet other players on Wreckers' Key and go out as a squad of up to four.
  • This is an early build. Things will break. The Report a problem button on the title screen makes one file you can send back.
  • Everyone playing together needs the same version. It is on the title screen. READ-ME-FIRST in the zip has the rest.
